A NEW KIND OF SCIENCE, UNDER UNIVALENCE
Coordinatization is a path, the merge is a truncation with no section,
the bounded observer separates no co-terminal runs, and the interchange
converges by refl

Multicomputation / observer theory / cubical type theory

Wolfram's programme rests on sentences that have until now been read as
programme rather than as mathematics.  We take four of them verbatim
and hold each to a machine-checked term, over the smallest multiway
system that exhibits all four phenomena: a unary substitution system
with two updating events — append one cell, append two — whose
multiway evolutions between fixed endpoints form an indexed inductive
family.  Nothing is argued by simulation and nothing is asserted of a
rule class; each sentence names a theorem, and the theorem's witness
is exhibited at named points.  Checked at Agda 2.8.0 with agda/cubical
v0.9, --cubical --safe, no postulates, no holes.

W1.  "Each [computational system] can be thought of as defining a
different coordinate system for describing the ruliad ... each will
ultimately lead to the same limiting object."  This is univalence,
used as Voevodsky built it.  The same system is presented in two
coordinate systems — unary tapes with cell-appending events, numbers
with successor events — and the coordinate change is an equivalence
that ua turns into a path in the universe.  The theorem is that the
two STRUCTURED systems, carrier together with both updating events,
are connected by a single path in the type of systems: the event
components ride over ua by ua→, and the commuting datum in each
component is refl, because the coding function sends a cell to a
successor definitionally.  Three corollaries discharge the sentence's
three clauses.  Every property of systems crosses the coordinate
change by transport, with no re-proof — the interchange law, proved in
tape coordinates by refl, is delivered in number coordinates by subst.
The emulation that "moves" between coordinate systems is not a map
postulated beside the identification: it is the path's own transport,
and it COMPUTES to the coding function, by uaβ — in cubical type
theory univalence is a theorem with computational content, which is
the entire difference between citing an equivalence and running one.
And "the same limiting object" is not agreement-after-inspection but
contractibility: the space of coordinatizations of the fixed carrier,
each carrying its own identification, is a point (EquivContr).  No
coordinate system is privileged, and the privilege's absence is a
contraction, not a convention.

W2.  "Whenever we said that two paths in the ruliad 'merge', that's
really just saying that we treat the outcomes as equivalent."  The
merge is propositional truncation, and its cost is a computed object.
Two evolutions from state 0 to state 3 — one-then-two, two-then-one —
are proved distinct by an invariant that reads the first updating
event, and proved merged by the squash.  The branchial pair is then
exhibited where this series always puts what an identification
destroys: as two distinct residents of the fibre of the merge over the
one merged point.  Branch structure is not what a finer observer would
see; it is the fibre of the coarser observer's map, and it is
computed, in the sense of abstract 07, where the same statement is
proved for a reversible process calculus.

W3.  "Because we are computationally bounded observers who imagine a
certain coherence in their experience, there are strong constraints on
what kinds of equivalence classes we can use."  The constraint is
quantified and the repair is refuted.  Any consumer of the merged
single thread — any function out of the truncation, into every type at
every universe level — answers equally on the two branches.  And the
merge has no section: a selector reading a run back out of the thread
collides with the branch distinction, so route information provably
cannot be reconstructed downstream and must be carried or lost.  This
is computational irreducibility in its proof-relevant form — the
outcome does not determine the run — and it is the multiway instance
of what abstract 06 proves for derivations (soundness factors through
the truncation, and the truncation is strict), abstract 22 for
denotations (full abstraction is the statement that the denotation IS
the truncation), and abstract 16 for program synthesis (pruning by
observational equivalence collapses a fibre that is constructed and
unbounded).

W4.  "Causal invariance can be thought of as being associated with
paths of history that diverge, eventually converging again."  From
every state, the two updating events diverge — one cell apart after
one event — and both orders complete to the same state three cells on,
with the endpoint agreement definitional: the two interchanged runs
inhabit the same type, with no transport and no path algebra.  Stated
exactly: what is checked is endpoint agreement of interchanged
co-initial updating events, the convergence the sentence names.  Where
the corpus already went further — abstract 14 proves that pointwise
pairwise commutation makes EVERY permutation of a run agree on every
source state, and that any observed order-dependence localises a state
outside the image of the observation — the citation is to that
theorem's actual content, agreement of states under reordering.

W5.  "We don't get any fundamental advantage by trying to make a proof
rather than just running the Turing machine" (Towards a Theory of
Bugs, July 2026).  The sentence is exactly right about each single
instance and exactly wrong about their totality, and both halves are
checked: a run reaches one endpoint, and the term (n : ℕ) → Evolve 0 n
reaches every endpoint by one induction through a snoc lemma.  The
fundamental advantage of proof is the universal quantifier — the term
is the family where the run is a point — and the corpus already holds
the machine-scale form in abstract 25's composition theorem, which
certifies a compound machine for every input with no induction over
the compound.  The same essay's "whatever constraint we give, there'll
always be machines that insidiously escape it" is this series'
standing discipline read back: the escape is a named counterexample,
and a constraint without its exhibited escape is an unfinished
theorem.

W6.  "Equivalent states are in fact merged" (What Ultimately Is
There?, February 2026).  The February sentence is stronger than the
2021 merge — merged in the object, not treated as equivalent by an
observer — and the stronger merge is the set quotient: under
co-terminality the two branches become equal AS DATA (eq/), the
quotient contracts to a single point, and that point is Wolfram's eme
— an atom of existence with no property beyond its distinctness —
constructed rather than posited.  Both merges are real, both lossy,
and the loss is the same computed object: the stronger merge still
has no section, by the same two-cong collision that refuted
reconstruction from the truncation.

W7.  Bulk orchestration and the rulial ensemble (What's Special about

abstract 12's theorem arrived at from the November 2025 vocabulary.
The orchestration half — mechanism-like patches fitted together, the

One methodological sentence from What Is Ruliology? (January 2026)
frames the whole series: mathematics "tends to get out of hand, with
even a very simple rule having behavior that can only be captured by
large amounts of obscure mathematics."  The modules above are the
measured reply: at the scale where ruliology's own sentences live —
two events, one branch point, one merge — the mathematics does not get
out of hand; it gets exactly as large as the sentence, and each of
seven sentences across five years of writings (2021–2026) is
discharged by identifiers whose proof text is shorter than its prose.
Where a sentence names something genuinely irreducible — the January
2025 window on the 307-step proof nobody understands — the corpus
agrees from the other side: abstract 08 proves that proof identity is
the fibre, so an ununderstood proof is a trace not yet carried, and
carrying traces is what this machine is for.

The machine scale of W1 is already in the corpus and is cited rather
than re-proved: abstract 25's universal machine, where codes modulo
running-the-same form a set-quotient, the padding lemma injects ℕ into
the codes of one single behaviour point, determinism is contractibility
of the space of productive runs, and the deterministic machine sits
strictly inside the interactive one with the strictness measured by
the event type.  What this module adds is the coordinatization
sentence itself, as one path of structured systems whose transport
computes — the sentence of Wolfram's that only univalence can hold,
because it identifies presentations while refunding, through
transport, everything the identification would otherwise owe.

The development is deliberately at the smallest scale that is not
degenerate: two updating events, one branch point, one merge, two
coordinate systems.  Every constructor is reachable by hand, every
refutation lands on a named collision, and the four sentences are
discharged by nine identifiers whose combined proof text is shorter
than this abstract.  The ruliad's load-bearing sentences are, one by
one, theorems of univalent mathematics.
