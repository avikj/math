# The Ceptr Written Corpus and Brock/Harris-Braun's Ceptr-Adjacent Essays: Annotated Bibliography

**Method note.** The Wayback Machine (web.archive.org, including the CDX API) was blocked from this environment (both curl and the fetch tool), so the enumeration was done instead from (a) the live ceptr.org site (which is still online, served from the Jekyll repo github.com/metacurrency/ceptr-web), (b) that site's sitemap.xml and full git history (374 commits, 2016-11-29 to 2024-06-12; the WordPress-era posts were imported into Jekyll on 2016-12-09, with original WordPress IDs/dates preserved in front-matter), (c) the related GitHub wikis (metacurrency/ceptr-org, zippy/ceptr, zippy/anansi), (d) the live artbrock.com archive (124 posts), (e) the live metacurrency.org, and (f) Google Docs that ceptr.org links to. Medium.com and ceptr.decko.org/ceptr.wagn.org ("Ceptr Core Dumps") returned Cloudflare 403s; items known only from those are listed with their URLs and marked as unverified. Everything marked "verified" below was read directly.

## Key Question 1: What is the complete list of Ceptr-related written items, with dates and URLs?

### Takeaway
The Ceptr written corpus proper consists of 19 ceptr.org blog posts (Sept 2014 - June 2017; 13 by Brock, 6 by Harris-Braun), 6 ceptr.org "project" pages and 6 "participate" pages (all created Jan-Dec 2017 in the Jekyll rebuild), 12 whitepaper stubs/pages (of which only three have substantial text: the Ceptr Revelation, Grammatic Capacities, and Mutual Credit Cryptocurrencies), plus a halo of Google Docs, GitHub wiki/README pages and ~30 artbrock.com and harris-braun.com essays that supply the conceptual program (MetaCurrency, current-sees, social DNA, grammatic/expressive capacity, SAC). No ceptr.org page titled "Ceptr IoT", "WordPress in Ceptr", "Pulse", "Ceptr ID" or "Ceptr Marketplace" exists in the live site, its sitemap or its git history; the closest equivalents are the "Compository" (code marketplace), "Streamscapes" (communications app), "Neural Internetworking" (listener/IoT-like sensing) and "Ceptr Identity and Web of Trust" (identity) material documented below.

Includes 2015-16 comments by Marc Clifton and Harris-Braun; the latter explains that "scapes" hold the relational fabric between units in "a geometrical substrate (of arbitrary topology)" and support "listenability" (a couch-surfing receptor planting a listener for available rooms). ## Key Question 3: What are the "layers" of the MetaCurrency stack (Open Rules/Transport/Data/Identity) and how does Ceptr implement each?

### Takeaway
MetaCurrency (2009-2010) defined four "open" elements — Open Identity, Open Rules, Open Transport, Open Data; the Revelation explicitly maps each to a Ceptr mechanism (contextual name resolution and triangulated UUIDs; executable self-describing protocols/receptors replacing XGFL; CeptrNet with no central address authority; signed chains + DHT = Intrinsic Data Integrity) and reframes "open" as "unenclosable."

### Cited Findings
- Original definitions (MetaCurrency site, quoted verbatim): "Open Identity: Create, manage and own your identity in a trustworthy manner, independent of any central authority. Open Rules: Know the rules of any currency you participate in and see when they change. Open Transport: A protocol to enable a participant to transact with any other participant and a currency to interact with any other currency. Open Data: The ability to share, decentralize and distribute data (like your account balance) and ensure its integrity and privacy… also, to allow you to be a reliable authority of your own data." — [P2P wiki Metacurrency Project](https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/Metacurrency_Project); also [artbrock.com/metacurrency/faq](https://www.artbrock.com/metacurrency/faq) ("MetaCurrency is the name for the infrastructure and protocols necessary for an open source economy"); [Mira Luna 2010](http://trustcurrency.blogspot.com/2010/03/metacurrency-project.html).
- 2009 precursor with three components (identity deferred): Open Transport, Open Rules, Open Data, "Identity is treated as a lesser issue, with OpenID suggested for now"; Open Data most emphasized — "integrity should be built into the data structure itself through linked, digitally signed transactions" — [Differences Between Open Source and Open Currency, 2009-05-12](https://www.artbrock.com/2009/05/12/differences-between-open-source-and-open-currency); Intrinsic Data Integrity origin — [Footprints of Flow, 2009-03-21](https://www.artbrock.com/2009/03/21/the-footprints-of-flow).
- Ceptr's mapping (Revelation, "Make it 'Unenclosable'"): "The original MetaCurrency web site explained what we were building in four simple elements": **Open Identity** — "We never wrote much about our design for identity. Our plan was to borrow a lot from... XDI/XRI/iNames. Ceptr's identity model is similar... with the biggest differences being that we use contextual name resolution instead of having a global top to the namespace"; **Open Transport** — the Internet's central address/name authority, ISP monopolies, DNS root servers and "kill switch" show "we need an even more open infrastructure that could operate completely peer-to-peer"; **Open Data** — "Adapting ideas from Ian Grigg about triple-entry accounting and Ricardian Contracts... decentralized data models with signed transaction chains to ensure Intrinsic Data Integrity" (git and bitcoin as popular variants); **Open Rules** — "a format for sharing the rules, actions and actors in a currency... We initially implemented this with XGFL (eXtensible Game Format Language). You could drop an XGFL spec into a flowplace... We then discovered the issues of interoperability between rule-sets were much more complex than could be sensibly addressed by an expressive capacity akin to XML." Conclusion: "open" means "universal... unenclosable," hence P2P — [Revelation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Line362Wm0zMOZcEZMqPYfHqNS4XIVyVsP7SS_4jE2o/edit).
- Ceptr implementation of each: Identity -> CeptrNet "addresses organized around agency and accountability," self-generated UUIDs "validated via triangulation process of trusted peers," progressive trust, capabilities tokens, DPKI — [Web of Trust brief](https://ceptr.org/blog/2015-11-03-distributed-receptor-based-computing), [Ceptr Core](https://ceptr.org/projects/core); Rules -> protocols as "live, executable" components with slots, Expectations, and receptors that install unknown protocols ("Protocols as live, pluggable code rather than as specifications") — [Pcubed](https://ceptr.org/projects/pcubed), [First Dev Post](https://ceptr.org/blog/2016-03-30-musings-on-coding-in-ceptr-first-dev-post), with versioning/deprecation "right into the low-level design" — [Revelation]; Transport -> CeptrNet as a receptor with "no centralized address or name authority," front-end vs back-end protocols, DHT-style overlay routing — [Ceptr Core](https://ceptr.org/projects/core); Data -> "Intrinsic Data Integrity" (countersigned hash chains, Merkle trees, sharded DHT with authors keeping copies), later isolated as Holochain — [Holochain project page](https://ceptr.org/projects/holochain), [Restructuring post](https://ceptr.org/blog/2017-01-17-restructuring-ceptr-sub-projects).
- A later five-layer stack (2011, reported by Peter Vander Auwera from a MetaCurrency collabathon): "Stream-scapes... Decision making... Currencies... Holoptinets... OS earth" — [P2P wiki Metacurrency Project](https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/Metacurrency_Project).
- The 2017 sub-project decomposition is the operational form of the layers: Pcubed (protocols/rules), Holochain (data integrity), Holo (hosting/transport to edges), SAC (governance), Neural (listeners), Core (receptors, CeptrNet, Compository) — [ceptr.org](https://ceptr.org/).

### Inferences
- The layers were never a strict OSI-style stack; Ceptr collapses them into one substrate (self-describing semantic protocols over signed-chain data on an agency-addressed network), which is why Brock insists Ceptr cannot be assembled from existing tools.
- Open Rules is the layer whose first implementation (XGFL/flowplace) failed and whose redesign (protocol-for-protocols) became Ceptr's central idea.

### Gaps
- Primary 2009-2010 metacurrency.org pages are only available via quotations on the P2P wiki and artbrock.com FAQ; the "first design document for the MetaCurrency platform" the P2P wiki links was not retrieved.

## Key Question 4: How do "current-sees", "social DNA", "agreement engines", and "holopticism" connect into the Ceptr architecture?

### Takeaway
In the corpus, *current-sees* are the symbol systems that make flows visible, *social DNA* is the encoded agreement-set (currencies/protocols) that every member of a social organism carries, and *holopticism* (Noubel's term) is the whole-visibility that current-sees provide; Ceptr is explicitly built as the "grammatic capacity" for composing all three — each participant runs a receptor containing the group's agreements "like DNA in cells," signed data makes flows traceable, and listeners/scapes/holoptinets render the whole. The phrase "agreement engine" does not occur in any located Brock/Harris-Braun text; the equivalent concept is "executable agreements"/SAC/"pluggable governance."

### Cited Findings
- Current-sees: "formal information systems that allow communities to interact with flows... trade currencies, reputation currencies, loyalty currencies, performance metric currencies" and the goal "to create a new expressive capacity, a 'flow mechanics'" — Harris-Braun quoted on [P2P wiki Metacurrency Project](https://wiki.p2pfoundation.net/Metacurrency_Project); "symbols that make flows visible" with token and system life cycles — [Intro to Currency Design](https://www.artbrock.com/2017/02/27/intro-to-currency-design); current-sees analysed by issuer/redeemer/life cycle — [Current-see and Death Straight Talk](https://eric.harris-braun.com/blog/2016/02/16/current-see-and-death-straight-talk/); currencies as "dynamic maps, with flowing symbols showing the movement of resources, pollution, carbon, access"; Ceptr supplies "the basic building blocks for composing the real 'current-sees'" — [Why Ceptr?](https://www.artbrock.com/2015/11/12/why-ceptr-or-transcending-maslows-hierarchy); Holochain enables "whole ecosystems of interoperable current-sees (monetary and non-monetary)" — [Explorations](https://ceptr.org/participate/explorations).
- Social DNA: currencies are the DNA of social organisms and "changing the people" does not change the organism — [Currencies as DNA, 2009](https://www.artbrock.com/2009/04/26/currencies-as-dna-of-social-organisms); re-encode social DNA by redesigning currencies, "the formal encoding of flows" — [Deepest Importance, 2011](https://www.artbrock.com/2011/07/21/the-deepest-importance-of-currencies); "current-see design... to consciously design the DNA of social organisms" — [MetaCurrency Myth](https://www.artbrock.com/2016/02/27/the-metacurrency-myth). ### Inferences
- The chain of reasoning in the corpus is: social organisms are real living systems -> their DNA is their currencies/agreements -> redesigning them needs a new expressive (grammatic) capacity -> that capacity must be unenclosable and distributed -> hence receptors carrying agreements, signed chains, self-describing protocols, and holoptic listeners.
- "Holoptinets" names the intended end-user layer for holopticism (visualization of flows at scale) and was never written beyond a title.

## Key Question 5: What did the 2015-2016 "Ceptr Platform" strategy documents say about go-to-market (developer community, Ceptr Foundation, licensing)?

### Takeaway
The located strategy material (Nov 2015 - June 2017) describes a non-profit, do-ocratic MetaCurrency Project that planned to: launch Ceptr via Bay Area events in January 2016; document heavily and widen the developer community (dev chats, hackathons, do-ops, residencies); govern the code commons through a Sovereign Accountable Commons / "Source Tree Commons" rather than a conventional foundation; reward contributors with a royalties system and a mutual-credit "Ceptr Fuel" redeemable for hosting; and only in mid-2017 accept investment and explore an ICO while "preserv[ing] the integrity of our commitment to open source." No document naming a "Ceptr Foundation" or a specific software license for Ceptr was found; the licensing question was addressed later for Holochain (Cryptographic Autonomy License, 2018-19).

### Inferences
- There was no "Ceptr Foundation": the governance design was deliberately a self-hosted SAC/Source Tree Commons plus a fuel currency, and the 2017 investment structure plus Holo (for-profit) became the practical substitute.
- Go-to-market shifted from "release the whole Ceptr stack" (2015-16) to "lead with Holochain as a blockchain alternative" (2017), with Pcubed/Core deferred.
