Primary-source index

This is the external-dependency index for the lineage account. The paper's checked terms remain the authority for claims proved on the page; these sources locate imported interfaces and historical formulations.

Reduction, proof, computability

  • Church & Rosser (1936), “Some Properties of Conversion.” Confluence / Church–Rosser. Paper: §5.11 contrast; §5.18.
  • Curry (1934); Howard (1969 manuscript, published 1980), propositions as types. Proof/program correspondence. Paper: §5.1–5.3.
  • Turing (1936), “On Computable Numbers, with an Application to the Entscheidungsproblem.” Effective computation and halting obstruction. Paper: §4.10.
  • Lawvere (1969), “Diagonal Arguments and Cartesian Closed Categories.” Abstract fixed-point/diagonal theorem. Paper: §4.2–4.15.

Equality, dependent and cubical type theory

  • Martin-Löf, dependent/intensional type theory (1970s onward). Identity types, J, dependent substitution. Paper: §0.
  • Hofmann & Streicher (1998), “The groupoid interpretation of type theory.” Nontrivial intensional identity structure.
  • Voevodsky / Univalent Foundations. Univalence: equivalence as identity of types. Paper: §2.4.
  • Bezem, Coquand & Huber (2014), “A Model of Type Theory in Cubical Sets.” Cubical model with computational path structure.
  • Cohen, Coquand, Huber & Mörtberg, “Cubical Type Theory: a constructive interpretation of the univalence axiom” (preprint 2016; publication 2018). De Morgan interval, composition, Glue, computational univalence. Paper: §§0–3.

Optimal sharing and local interaction

  • Jean-Jacques Lévy (1970s), labelled lambda calculus; Réductions correctes et optimales dans le lambda-calcul (1978); “Le problème du partage dans l'évaluation des lambda-expressions” (1978). Residuals/families and optimal sharing. Paper: §5.24 lineage.
  • Girard (1987), “Linear Logic”; Geometry of Interaction papers. Explicit resource structure, proof nets, execution paths.
  • Lamping (1990), “An Algorithm for Optimal Lambda Calculus Reduction.” Local sharing-graph implementation of Lévy optimality. Paper: §5.24 lineage.
  • Lafont (1990), “Interaction Nets.” Principal ports, active pairs, local interaction; direct source for §5.11 geometry.
  • Lafont (1997), “Interaction Combinators.” Universal local interaction calculus.
  • Asperti & Guerrini (1998), The Optimal Implementation of Functional Programming Languages. Comprehensive optimal-sharing synthesis.
  • Asperti, Giovannetti & Naletto (1996), BOHM. Bologna Optimal Higher-order Machine implementation.

Information and path cost

  • Shannon (1948), “A Mathematical Theory of Communication.” Information/conditional entropy vocabulary. Paper: §3.3.
  • Landauer (1961), “Irreversibility and Heat Generation in the Computing Process.” Erasure/thermodynamic bound. Paper: §3.4, §5.9.
  • Bennett (1973 and later), reversible computation. Reversible organization of computation.
  • Bellman, dynamic programming / principle of optimality. Potential/distance formulation used at §5.15, §8.11, §9.13.
  • Huffman (1952), “A Method for the Construction of Minimum-Redundancy Codes.” Two-smallest merge rule / weighted path length. Paper: §9.10.
