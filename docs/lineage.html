The construction before this page

A chronological account of the modern formal interfaces consumed by Respecting Equality. Provenance, not authority: the proofs are on the main page.

1. Proof becomes computation

1930sChurch–Rosser: reduction has a unique destination

Lambda calculus makes substitution computation: (λx.M)N contracts to M[x:=N]. Church–Rosser gives confluence, so a normal form, when it exists, is unique. This is weaker than §5's property: beta-redexes can overlap, so beta-reduction lacks the one-step diamond from which equal trace length follows.

Paper: 5.11, 5.18.

1934–1969Curry–Howard: proof and program are one syntax

proposition ↔ type proof ↔ term normalization ↔ evaluation

Logical construction and typed term construction are one compositional structure. The requested type—not a different engine—distinguishes proving, deciding, computing and returning a value.

Paper: 5.1–5.3.

2. The many diagonals become one

1930sGödel, Church, Turing: formal systems become input

Gödel arithmetizes syntax; Church and Turing formalize effective procedure and expose its limits. Proofs, programs and machines become mathematical data, making self-application exact rather than rhetorical.

1969Lawvere: diagonalization is one fixed-point theorem

(∀h:A→B. Σa. e a ≡ h) ⇒ (∀g:B→B. Σb. g b ≡ b)

If a table contains every row, every endomap of its codomain has a fixed point; the proof needs only the row a ↦ g(e a a). Fixed-point-free choices give Cantor, Russell, Tarski and halting; read positively, the same construction gives self-reference. Gödel additionally requires representability and the stated consistency hypotheses.

Paper: 4.2–4.15.

3. Equality becomes executable

1970sMartin-Löf: equality is internal data

Dependent type theory internalizes equality; J is dependent substitution, and transport is its specialization.

p : x ≡ y ⇒ transport p : P x → P y

The paper goes beneath the interface: §0 explicitly contracts Σ[y∈A](x≡y) to (x,refl) and obtains J by transport from the centre.

Paper: 0.8–0.10.

1998Hofmann–Streicher: identity has structure

The groupoid interpretation shows intensional identity need not collapse to proof-irrelevant equality: identity witnesses can compose and invert nontrivially, opening the semantic route to paths and higher paths.

2000sVoevodsky: equivalence is identity of types

(A ≃ B) ≃ (A ≡ B)

Univalence identifies equivalence with identity of types. As an axiom it does not itself tell a normalizer how arbitrary univalence terms compute; the next problem is to make the identification run.

2014Bezem–Coquand–Huber: cubical equality

Cubical semantics gives equality formal dimensions: p : 𝕀 → A with endpoints p 0=x, p 1=y; composition/filling supplies coherent extension.

2016/2018CCHM: univalence computes

e : A ≃ B ua(e) : A ≡ B transport(ua(e)) = e

The De Morgan interval, composition/filling and Glue construct an identification whose transport is the equivalence. This is the final imported equality interface: the paper begins where this leaves off.

Paper: 2.4 → 3.2.

4. Substitution becomes identity of work

1970sLévy: two redexes can be one computation

(λx. x x) R → R R

If R contains a redex, two reducible occurrences appear. A residual is what remains of a redex after another contraction; following residuals through copying gives genealogy. Lévy's families identify occurrences with one computational origin.

redex occurrence ≠ family of work

Lévy optimality means family work is not redundantly performed; it does not mean minimum machine instructions or wall time.

Paper: prior notion behind 5.24; distinct from equal length at 5.13.

1987Girard: duplication becomes structure

Linear logic makes copying/discarding explicit resource operations; proof nets expose normalization graphically and Geometry of Interaction emphasizes execution paths. Proof nets, sharing graphs and interaction nets remain distinct constructions.

1990Lamping: Lévy sharing becomes local

Lévy supplies the criterion; Lamping supplies a local graph implementation. Fan/sharing structure records duplication history so common work need not be blindly copied, while still allowing copies to separate when their contexts require it.

global residual genealogy → local sharing graph

Paper: immediate lineage of 5.24.

1996–1998Asperti–Guerrini / BOHM

The Optimal Implementation of Functional Programming Languages assembles optimal reduction, Lamping sharing graphs, readback/correctness, proof-net/GoI interpretations, complexity, Interaction Systems and implementation; BOHM carries the program into an executable optimal higher-order machine.

Lévy families → Lamping graphs → correctness/readback → machine

Its optimality remains Lévy's; §5's equal-length result is an additional theorem about reduction geometry.

5. Computation becomes local interaction

1990Lafont: interaction nets

A cell has one principal port; a redex is two principal ports joined as an active pair. Two distinct active pairs therefore cannot share a cell. Reducing one leaves the other unchanged, so either two proposed steps are the same or they reconverge after one further step each.

s → a, s → b ⇒ a ≡ b or ∃c. a → c ← b

This one-step diamond is stronger than ordinary confluence. §5 spends exactly that strength:

principal-port locality → disjoint active pairs → diamond → peel → equal normalizing length → minimality

Paper: 5.4–5.5, 5.11–5.18.

1997Lafont: interaction combinators

Universal distributed computation compresses to three agent kinds and six local rules. The point here is not the count: general computation can be lowered to a tiny algebra executed entirely by local active-pair interactions.

6. Cost separates into loss and distance

1948–1982Shannon, Landauer, Bennett

Information loss receives an exact measure and physical price; reversible computation can avoid erasure except where information is actually discarded. The paper first proves the abstract algebraic statement, with the physical reading downstream:

ℓ(g)+ℓ(g⁻¹)=ℓ(id)=0 ⇒ ℓ(g)=0

Paper: 3.3–3.6, 5.7–5.10.

dynamic programmingBellman: a potential certifies distance

Φ(u) ≤ 1 + Φ(v)

A potential satisfying this on every unit edge lower-bounds remaining distance; equality on each edge of a route certifies a geodesic. Thus the paper has two different routes to optimality: diamond ⇒ all normalizing routes equal length; exact potential ⇒ geodesicity even without the diamond.

Paper: 5.15–5.17, 8.11, 9.13.

1952Huffman: representation fixes weighted path cost

Combining the two smallest weights minimizes weighted external path length. At unit weights this is the balanced sum minimizing the sum of intermediate sums in §9.

Paper: 9.10.

7. Where the lines meet

CCHM supplies A ≃ B → A ≡ B with transport computing as the equivalence. The paper proves reversible operation = transport. Its fibre equivalence A ≃ Σ[b]f⁻¹(b) keeps output and preimage together; truncation is precisely forgetting which witness. Lawvere supplies the obstruction when total self-description would force a forbidden fixed point.

On execution, Lévy/Lamping identify and preserve shared work; Lafont supplies the one-step diamond; §5 derives equal normalizing length and minimality rather than importing an optimality slogan. The later equalities continue the same maps:

ℕ = {finite sets}/≅ gcd/lcm = meet/join on prime-exponent vectors σ² = −1 F=E+iB : Maxwell vacuum evolution ⇔ i∂ₜF=∇×F solutions = F⁻¹(true); decision = truncation of that fibre |sort⁻¹(s)| = n! ∏merges C(a+b,a) = n! distance to sorted order = inversion count

The chronology ends here because the remaining statements are proved on the page. The point is not that separate fields are “applied” to one another; it is that the modern literature had already supplied these interfaces, after which their distinctions can be tested by explicit maps.
