# Cubical Stokes, valuation and the operational realization

Author addendum, 17 October 2026. Cumulative with FOUNDATION-ROUTE.md, EFFECT-AUTHORITY.md and LIVE-CUT-ADOPTION.md in the V23 review. This makes explicit the cubical-to-chain-to-execution interface those documents require. It preserves their implementation and effect rules. It does not assert that the new interface has already been implemented or that a whole-machine optimality theorem follows from conservation alone.

The source is the existing typed cubical construction. Chains, cochains, port incidence and work are its specified readings. They must not replace it with a graph-only semantics or a separate scalar ledger. The following equations are mathematical obligations; the proposed immutable descriptors and job decomposition are a concrete realization using the existing scopes, exact arithmetic, references, receipts and shared jobs.

1. THE CUBE CATEGORY IS THE SUBSTITUTION LANGUAGE

For a finite ordered dimension context I, let D(I) be its free De Morgan algebra: 0, 1, variables, meet, join and involution, subject to the De Morgan and distributive-lattice equations. Do not add i ∧ ~i = 0 or i ∨ ~i = 1. Faces use the existing face algebra, with its own entailment and endpoint equations.

A cube map σ:J→I assigns each coordinate in I an element of D(J). Composition is simultaneous substitution, not successive evaluation of the assigned images. An n-cube x reindexes contravariantly:

    x[id] = x
    (x[σ])[τ] = x[σ∘τ].

Faces insert 0 or 1; degeneracies forget a coordinate; exchanges permute coordinates; reversal uses ~; connections use ∧ or ∨; diagonals may identify coordinates. All are existing interval maps. There is no maximum cube dimension in the mathematical interface.

For a fixed typed parameter context Γ, base guard κ and ambient type A, a presented n-cell is a checked scope with n dimension locals and body in A, available throughout the cube under κ. A varying family is retained with its dependent boundary; to take ordinary constant-coefficient chains, use its total-space presentation or explicitly supply the coefficient transport in §6. A partial tube is a partial cell with an explicit face extent. It is not admitted as a total cell by treating the unprovided faces as zero.

At the representation boundary use a root scope and a canonical simultaneous dimension map, retaining annotations, parameter images, support and proof premises. Two successive restrictions compose into that map. Thus the two computations of a corner use the same coordinate assignment. Do not ask arbitrary program conversion to rediscover the cubical identities each time. Independently presented roots can remain different presented cells until an explicit comparison is supplied; their underlying core computations still use the ordinary structural sharing machinery.

The cubical face identity, for i<j, is

    d_i^ε d_j^η x = d_(j−1)^η d_i^ε x.

It is the equality of the two substitutions selecting the same codimension-two face. This identity is the entire cancellation mechanism behind boundary-of-boundary. The scope validation and simultaneous-map rules already implement its substrate.

2. THE ADDITIVE READING AND ITS DEGREE

Let X be this cubical presentation. Start with the unnormalized chain groups

    C_n(X) = free abelian group on the presented n-cells.

A chain is a finite sparse combination of exact signed integer coefficients and cell references. Finite means arbitrarily finite subject to actual storage, not bounded by a native bitmask. Identical presented generators combine; distinct cells are not identified just because their printed endpoints agree. Zero coefficients disappear from the chain reading, not from the retained source evidence or occurrence history.

For n>0 define

    ∂x = Σ_(i=1)^n (−1)^(i−1) (d_i^1 x − d_i^0 x),
    ∂(Σ a_x x) = Σ a_x ∂x.

Set ∂:C_0→0. In ∂²x, each pair of coordinates occurs in both deletion orders. The face identity identifies the resulting face and the signs are opposite. Therefore ∂²=0 in every dimension, with no per-dimension theorem or special case beyond n=0.

If normalized chains are requested, their meaning is C_n/D_n, where D_n is the subgroup generated by degeneracies. The degeneracy identities make D_* a subcomplex. This quotient is a specified mathematical reading; it never deletes actual operational events. Keeping a witness of degeneracy avoids confusing an unavailable conversion decision with proof of nondegeneracy. The unnormalized representation is sufficient for the identities and execution contracts below; normalization is not a new admission requirement.

Independent products carry the product orientation. For x of degree p and y of degree q,

    ∂(x×y) = (∂x)×y + (−1)^p x×(∂y).

The sign is forced by placing the p coordinates of x before the q coordinates of y. Product formation and this boundary traversal are scoped constructions over the same references.

3. COCHAINS, STOKES AND WHAT AN IDENTITY CHANGES

For an abelian coefficient group M, define

    C^n(X;M) = Hom(C_n(X),M),
    (da)(c) = a(∂c).

An executable cochain supplies its value on a presented generator and extends by the group operations. Evaluation can suspend if that value is being computed. The pairing is finite when its input chain is finite; it does not enumerate all cells of X. Coefficients need not be one scalar currency. Exact integers, tuples and finitely supported modules over named units are instances.

The defining equation is Stokes:

    <a,∂c> = <da,c>,       d²=0.

For a 0-cochain v, dv(e)=v(target e)−v(source e). For a 1-cochain a and a supplied 2-cell h comparing histories γ and γ', with the full boundary interpretation ∂h=γ'−γ,

    a(γ')−a(γ) = <da,h>.

This says exactly what is unchanged by an identity and, when a reading changes, what its defect is. If da vanishes on the admitted comparison cells, a descends through their additive relations. If it does not vanish, the source retains the defect with that comparison. For an additive quotient by a specified relation subgroup, annihilating that subgroup is necessary and sufficient for descent.

For a 3-cell k comparing composites of comparisons,

    <d(da),k> = 0.

Thus the signed defects around its boundary sum to zero. Coherence is dimensional, not an extra case-by-case global consistency check. It applies when the stated higher cell exists; arbitrary incompatible agency choices do not acquire a filler by declaration.

4. CYCLES, QUOTIENTS AND THE RETAINED FIBRE

Write Z_n=ker ∂ and B_n=im ∂. Then B_n⊆Z_n and H_n=Z_n/B_n. These are different predicates:

    a cycle has no boundary;
    a boundary comes with a higher chain witnessing that boundary;
    an observation-invisible construction is killed by that observation.

The last predicate depends on the observation. It is not interchangeable with either of the first two. The machine must not turn ∂z=0 into z=0 or fabricate a filler.

A closed cochain evaluates equally on homologous cycles. An exact cochain evaluates to zero on every cycle. A closed cochain can have a nonzero period on a nonbounding cycle. On a graph, potentials are determined only up to a constant on each connected component; H^0 records those independent constants. A flow that is a potential difference has zero circulation on every loop. Passing from local zero-defect equations to a global potential additionally requires the periods on the remaining cycles to vanish, with witnesses in the constructive interface.

For any observation q:S→B, the lossless construction remains

    S ≃ Σ(b:B).fib_q(b).

Hence a history can return to its initial balance, boundary observation or semantic value while retaining different work, acquired facts, receipts and commitments in the fibre. Quotienting a chosen reading is permitted. Deleting the source information needed by another reading is not. The repo's meaning-equal/cost-distinct histories and positive-cost reversal round trips are direct instances of this distinction.

5. BASE CHANGE, MAPS AND SUBSTITUTION

A map of cubical objects f:X→Y commutes with every cube restriction. Its additive extension F satisfies

    F∂ = ∂F.

Pullback of cochains is f*a=a∘F, and

    d(f*a) = f*(da),
    <f*a,c> = <a,Fc>.

These are the algebraic conditions for a change of representation, contextual substitution or checked network interpretation to preserve the reading. Verify them on generators and use the shared extension, rather than re-proving them for each assembled execution.

Two different uses of substitution must be kept correctly typed. Substitution of parameters induces maps between the corresponding cubical families and hence chain maps. A cube-coordinate substitution, such as restricting an n-cube to a face or diagonal, changes the degree of the resulting cube. It is not by itself a degree-zero chain endomorphism on all of C_*(X). Do not implement the false rule 'every interval reindex commutes with ∂ as a same-degree operation'. The face formula and the cubical identities state the correct relationship.

For dependent families, retain the pullback family and its transport rather than comparing fields at unrelated types. Face-system pieces are checked on their own guards and agree, with the required paths, on overlaps. A filler is produced by the actual cubical composition rule when its premises hold. ∂²=0 is not an unrestricted filling oracle for arbitrary closed shells.

This is the relevant algebraic-geometric organization: coordinate algebras, contravariant substitution, families over a base, restriction to faces, compatible gluing, and descent of specified readings. The higher typed construction carries the geometry; its chain complex is an additive projection.

6. DEPENDENT VALUATIONS AND LOCAL COEFFICIENTS

If a valuation takes values in varying groups M_x, addition requires an explicit comparison between fibres. Use a coefficient system supplying group isomorphisms along the relevant paths, respecting identity, composition and the admitted 2-cell comparisons. This is a local system on the chosen cubical presentation. Do not silently identify two agents' units or install a universal scalar exchange rate.

Choose the all-zero vertex of a cube as the coefficient location. For a coefficient m at that vertex, the lower i-face has the same base vertex. The upper i-face has a shifted base vertex; let T_i be transport along the corresponding edge. Then

    ∂(m·x) = Σ_i (−1)^(i−1)
                (T_i(m)·d_i^1 x − m·d_i^0 x).

The supplied square coherence equates the two transports to each codimension-two corner, so the same cancellation proof gives ∂²=0. For a cochain whose face values lie at each face's base vertex,

    (da)(x) = Σ_i (−1)^(i−1)
                (T_i^−1(a(d_i^1 x)) − a(d_i^0 x)).

If the proposed transports do not satisfy the required square coherence, this is not a flat coefficient system on those cells. Retain the actual comparison/defect in the typed source; do not claim that this twisted differential squares to zero. General dependent typed valuations remain available without pretending they already land in an abelian local system.

7. CUBICAL STATE BOUNDARIES AND PORT BOUNDARIES

The current documents use two compatible boundary readings. Their objects must be named explicitly.

In the execution presentation, degree-zero generators are complete machine states/cuts; directed executed steps are degree-one generators; admitted comparisons of step histories are degree-two generators; coherence of those comparisons supplies higher cells. A geometric path may describe a reversible equality without itself being an executed C trace. Its execution interpretation is an additional retained witness, not a free identification of these objects.

In the resource incidence complex, degree-zero generators are anchored port occurrences and degree-one generators are accepted local events:

    ∂_res[e] = Σ produced ports − Σ consumed ports.

Define B_0(s) to be the sum of the exposed port occurrences at cut s. Define B_1 on a primitive accepted transition to be its event, and on a pure transition that changes no resource cut to be zero. A resource-changing administrative transition must be interpreted explicitly rather than classified as pure. The required equation is

    ∂_res B_1(e) = B_0(target e) − B_0(source e).

This is the degree-one chain-map condition. Extending it linearly over a finite history gives the cut theorem. Internal port occurrences cancel because the very same occurrence is produced and consumed, not because two equal-looking payloads happen to print alike.

For a higher comparison, supply its corresponding resource cell or a certificate that its mapped boundary is zero. Declaring the resource complex concentrated in degrees 0 and 1 is valid only for comparisons whose mapped boundaries are zero. Comparisons that preserve final balances but change accepted event histories need the richer interpretation or must remain distinct there. A zero scalar reading does not manufacture equality of resource histories.

If a face of a comparison represents a composite execution, retain its actual occurrence chain. A single singular path for a concatenation is not literally the sum of its pieces in the free singular-chain group. The execution interpretation must carry the subdivision/composition witness or use the presented execution chain with its declared boundary. This prevents geometric equality from silently discarding implementation work.

8. OPEN CUTS, RELATIVE CHAINS AND GLUING

For an explicitly retained interface subcomplex A⊆X, relative chains are C_*(X,A)=C_*(X)/C_*(A). A region with boundary in A is a relative cycle. This is the precise reading of a subsystem whose remaining obligations lie on its interface. Its absolute boundary is still retained and is what another subsystem must match.

Given regions glued along an actual common interface J with opposite orientations, the internal boundary contributions cancel. When two retained histories overlap in the same actual events, their union uses inclusion-exclusion:

    [H_1∪_J H_2] = [H_1]+[H_2]−[J].

This equality is about the specified shared occurrence complex. It does not identify independent repeats of the same computation or effect. Two consumers can share a producer history and still contribute separate demand/delivery events.

A pending transfer and a suspended owned continuation remain on the open cut. Relative-cycle language cannot be used to erase them from absolute ownership. A partial valuation over one party or region reads the flux crossing that region's boundary. No global total chronology is needed for the cancellation proof.

The local reservation/commit rules in EFFECT-AUTHORITY.md are a realization of admissible incidence events and unique consumption. Conservation follows by composition once these premises hold. Stokes alone does not create the exclusion premise; accepting two conflicting consumes would fail to describe one permitted occurrence history in the first place.

9. POSITIVE EXECUTION WORK AND ORIENTED DIFFERENCES

There are three explicit readings:

    actual histories: a directed category of performed occurrences;
    additive comparison: signed chains of those occurrences/presented rules;
    semantic quotient: the chosen observation that may identify histories.

Actual work is nonnegative and additive along a performed sequence. Extend its weights to signed chains to compare histories. Algebraic −e is formal subtraction, not permission to execute e backward for negative work. If a reverse operation is actually run, it is its own directed occurrence e_rev, with its own nonnegative work. The repo's reversal round trip therefore keeps its positive cost even when its semantic reading is trivial.

For a comparison h with complete execution boundary γ'−γ, the identity defect is

    Δ_h W = W(γ')−W(γ) = <ω,∂h> = <dω,h>.

The complete state includes caches, facts, premises, authority and outstanding work used by the comparison. A result-only path may require a connector before it becomes a comparison at this boundary; connector work is included. Zero-defect scheduling cells yield equal work where the actual rules supply them. A nonzero-defect shortcut retains its exact shift. Higher coherence constrains composites of those shifts through d²=0.

Specializing symbolic pure-work supports uses Boolean direct image followed by deduplication. Pushing an integer chain forward uses additive multiplicities. These are different readings of the retained source and must not share an implementation that silently changes one into the other. Actual previously executed occurrences remain distinct unless they are literally the same historical events.

10. EXECUTABLE INTERFACE ON THE EXISTING MACHINE

Use immutable descriptors with core-reference fields and the existing shared resumable operation protocol. No new primitive datatype, separate interpreter or eager global complex is required. Each record retains its exact levels, contexts, guards, dependencies and evidence.

    CubeView(root_scope, dimension_map, parameter_map, boundary_signature)
    Chain(degree, shared sparse terms of CellRef × SignedExact)
    Cochain(degree, coefficient_signature, generator_action, law_witnesses)
    ChainMap(source, target, generator_action, boundary_witnesses)
    Realization(typed_cell, operational_boundary, map_witness, occurrence_links)
    RelativeView(chain, interface_inclusion, retained_absolute_boundary)

These names describe payloads of existing descriptors/templates, not permission to duplicate the current scope, map, term, checker or numeric infrastructure. A demanded operation is shared by its immutable operands and necessary dependency snapshot. An unevaluated result is an ordinary retained reference.

Operations and exact replacements:

* FACE(C,i,ε): compose the endpoint assignment into C's dimension map; retain the same root and other parameter images; return the n−1 cell view. No evaluation is needed to perform the substitution.
* CUBE_REINDEX(C,σ): compose simultaneous dimension maps; validate the resulting guard/signature through existing requests. Composition does not reindex map images twice.
* BOUNDARY(Σ a_x x): retain a cursor over input terms and coordinates. Emit the upper and lower face references with coefficients a_x(−1)^(i−1) and −a_x(−1)^(i−1). Combine by the existing exact shared map/set operations. Traversing n coordinates takes a retained sequence, never one unbounded transition.
* PAIR(a,Σ m_x x): request a(x) once per shared generator action and combine m_x·a(x) in the declared coefficient object. The evaluator checks those operations ordinarily. Pending terms preserve the accumulation frame.
* COB(a): return the cochain closure c↦PAIR(a,BOUNDARY(c)). Never enumerate the entire domain. d²=0 is a generic admitted law, instantiated through the same runtime identity mechanism.
* MAP_CHAIN(F,Σ m_x x): request the supplied generator images, scale and add them. Admission includes the boundary-compatibility witnesses. Their compositions are shared templates.
* PRODUCT, RELATIVE and GLUE: preserve their defining source cells/interfaces and expose the product-boundary, quotient or matching-interface reading lazily. A quotient certificate is required before exposing an erased distinction as equality in the chosen reading.
* PUBLISH_REALIZATION: link the already accepted typed step/cell, its exact operational boundary, retained occurrence identities and work. Publication cannot invent an executed event or a missing geometric filler.
* READ_DEFECT(a,h): evaluate PAIR(a,BOUNDARY(h)), equivalently PAIR(COB(a),h), with the shared Stokes witness. Existing literal/runtime-derived identities use the same path.

For local coefficients, BOUNDARY and COB insert the declared transports from §6; the flatness witnesses supply the corner-identification step. All variable work, coefficient arithmetic, map traversal and proof requests participates in the existing fine-step/ownership protocol. Native directory/address widths do not bound degree or coefficient size.

11. REQUIRED INTEGRATION CHECKS

These exercise the generic definitions, not a separate numerical model disconnected from the machine.

1. On the same scope, two distinct face-deletion orders return the same canonically composed coordinate map, including under an outer parameter substitution. Check dimensions above 64 as well as low-dimensional examples.
2. A symbolic 3-cube's expanded ∂² cancels exactly. Instantiate the generic proof at further dimensions; preserve it as a shared witness rather than a test-derived theorem.
3. A symbolic 1×1 product has the four signed edges required by Leibniz. An oriented reversal does not cancel two actually performed reverse-direction occurrences in the work reading.
4. Pairing with ∂ agrees with the cochain differential for a nonconstant valuation, exact large coefficients, and a multi-component coefficient group.
5. A nontrivial dependent coefficient transport uses the upper-face transport and its square witness; reject a claimed flat interpretation whose square witness is absent/invalid.
6. A cycle with no supplied filling remains a cycle; a scalar-zero observation does not delete its occurrence/evidence fibre. Supply a filling and verify the corresponding Stokes equation without deleting the original witness.
7. Map a finite accepted execution into the resource complex. Its boundary is exactly the input/output cut, including pending transfers. Verify the map equation for primitive events and composition from the same records.
8. Two histories sharing one producer count it once in their union, while distinct deliveries remain distinct. Specialization deduplicates symbolic support but does not rewrite the actual past occurrence count.
9. Compare a zero-defect scheduling square and a witnessed nonzero-defect identity replacement. The latter keeps its shift; compatible higher-cell composition keeps the signed sum of defects.
10. Run the effect witness from EFFECT-AUTHORITY.md with these readings attached: runtime identity, pending shared prefix, accepted send, duplicate receive, conflicting consume, returning cycle, and exact common trace. Check both absolute and relative cuts.

These requirements are cumulative and immediate. They specify the missing connection without replacing the ongoing kernel work. The generic boundary, substitution, Stokes and descent arguments above settle the mathematical interface; implementation conformance still requires connecting the actual rule records to these maps. A global geodesic theorem requires its stated premises on the actual operational relation and cannot be inferred solely from ∂²=0.
